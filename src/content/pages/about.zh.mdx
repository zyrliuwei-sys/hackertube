## What this site is

HackerTube AI is an independent public archive of videos discussed on Hacker News. Its current catalog contains 6,454 videos across 150 topics, drawn from discussions spanning 2007–2026. It helps readers find a talk, follow a subject, and return to the conversation in which a video was mentioned. The archive includes programming, mathematics, engineering and science, alongside design, history and other interests represented in those discussions.

The catalog is a snapshot, not a record of every video ever submitted to Hacker News. A listing is evidence that a video appeared in the source collection, not an endorsement by Hacker News, its users as a whole, or this site's maintainer. Browsing, searching and saving a collection in your browser do not require an account.

## Where the data comes from

Entries are adapted from the public [HackerTube export](https://hackertube.org/about), which uses the [open-index Hacker News archive](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-index/hacker-news). The export is attributed under the [Open Data Commons Attribution License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). Discussion links point back to the public source so readers can check the surrounding context.

Video thumbnails and the embedded player are provided by YouTube; some thumbnail copies are served locally. The videos are hosted on YouTube, not on this site. We index and classify references to them rather than hosting or distributing video files. Availability, playback controls and changes to the original upload remain with YouTube and the uploader.

## How videos get picked

The source export scanned public Hacker News comments for YouTube links. Its documented inclusion rule required mentions by at least three different people in at least three different months. Mentions include recommendations, references, criticism and jokes, so a mention count must not be read as a count of positive votes.

Scripts handle importing, deduplication, catalog filtering and navigation. The source project's descriptions and classifications were assisted by AI and can contain mistakes. Our editorial selections focus on substantive explanations and useful connections between videos. Fixed learning routes are explicitly selected and ordered; the interactive learning-path generator uses AI to suggest a sequence from the catalog. Neither process establishes the accuracy of every statement in an original video. We do not describe the entire archive as individually watched or manually verified.

## Corrections

Send the page URL, the field that appears wrong, and a link to supporting evidence to our support address. Reports can cover a title, classification, description, broken player or discussion reference. We review requests as maintenance time allows; there is currently no guaranteed response deadline. A correction is published after the relevant source can be checked. For copyright or removal requests, follow the information on the [contact page](/contact).
