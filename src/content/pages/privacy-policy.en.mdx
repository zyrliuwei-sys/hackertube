## About this site

[HackerTube AI](https://hacker-tube.com) is an independent directory for discovering videos from the public HackerTube collection. Public search and browsing do not require an account. This page describes the services currently used by the directory.

## Browsing and search

Opening a page or searching sends a request to this site's hosting service. A search request includes your search terms, selected filters, and page number. Network requests also expose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser information, to the services receiving them. This page does not claim that the hosting service keeps no access logs.

## Saved videos and preferences

Saved video IDs are stored in your browser's `localStorage`. This collection belongs to that browser and is not an account-based or cross-device collection. You can remove individual saved videos through the site or clear this site's browser storage to remove the saved list. Clearing browser storage also removes locally saved preferences.

The site's interface may store language or appearance preferences in browser storage or cookies. Browser settings let you inspect or clear this data. Blocking browser storage can prevent saved videos and preferences from working.

## AI learning paths

When you choose to generate a learning path, your learning goal and selected
experience level are sent to this site's server. Your goal and a shortlist of
public video metadata are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to create the path.
Avoid including private or sensitive information in your goal. The feature does
not save your goal to an account or database. Up to 50 successful results can be
cached in server memory for one hour. Ordinary hosting request logs may still
apply. The five most recently generated or reopened paths, including their goals and selected levels, and completion progress are saved in this browser’s `localStorage`. They are not synced to your account. Use “Clear saved paths” on the learning-path page to remove saved routes; clearing site storage also removes local progress and video collections. Downloading a path saves a Markdown file on your own device.

## Video players and images

A YouTube player is loaded from `youtube-nocookie.com` after you select a video. Loading and using that player sends network requests to YouTube and its service providers. This domain does not make playback anonymous. YouTube controls the player and processes its requests under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Some video thumbnails are served by external image services, including YouTube's image service. Those services receive ordinary network requests when their images load, even if you have not opened a player. Images served directly by this site are requested from this site's hosting service.

Following a link to YouTube, Hacker News, the original HackerTube project, or another external website leaves this site. The destination's own privacy practices apply there.

## Public catalog information

Catalog descriptions, classifications, and discussion links come from the public [HackerTube export](https://hackertube.org/about), based on the [open-index Hacker News archive](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-index/hacker-news). The export is attributed under the [Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). Public discussion links can identify the original public commenter on Hacker News. They are source references, not private account information collected from visitors.

## Accounts

Account registration is available but is not required for browsing, search, saved videos or learning paths. Accounts store your email address, name, password hash and session records. Authentication uses a session cookie. Account records can include first-touch attribution, country, IP and language collected when registering. Account information is kept while the account is in use; contact us to request access or deletion. We do not currently offer paid video plans.

## Analytics and retention

The site uses a self-hosted Plausible service at `stats.cadcraft.org` and Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page traffic and performance. Their requests expose ordinary connection information to those services. The first-party operations system described below is separate from those aggregate reports.

Our `ops_visitor` cookie contains a signed random browser identifier and expires after one year from its most recent renewal. First-touch records contain the source hostname or campaign source, country, entry page and deployment version. If you register or sign in, the browser record can be associated with your account. These are browser counts, not a count of distinct people. We do not fingerprint devices.

Product events record page views, video-page opens, search result counts (including zero results), playback attempts, clicks to YouTube, saves/removals, and learning-path requests, outcomes and response times. Search terms, learning goals, video titles and IP addresses are not stored in these product-event rows. A playback attempt is not proof that a video was watched. Invalid or rate-limited learning-path requests are not counted as generation attempts.

Raw product events older than 90 days and first-touch records older than 365 days are removed during subsequent event recording, with cleanup attempted at most once an hour per running server instance. This does not delete separate account records or the independently operated analytics providers’ logs. Local video collections and learning paths remain until you clear them. The server may store attribution cookies for 30 days and an analytics choice cookie for one year.

Our first-party visitor, event and attribution collection honors Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and the choice below. Turning off analytics also removes existing attribution cookies in this browser and prevents their values from being copied into a new account. Turning off tracking applies to future requests and does not erase earlier records. Browser settings can block or delete cookies; external services such as YouTube have their own controls. Cloudflare’s edge-injected analytics is controlled separately by the hosting provider, so this choice does not claim to disable every network request.
