## The service

[HackerTube AI](https://hacker-tube.com) is an independent video discovery and navigation site. It provides search, categories, source links, browser-local saved videos, and embedded playback for videos in the public HackerTube collection.

This edition does not host, download, or edit the videos themselves. Playback is provided by YouTube. Public search and browsing do not require an account.
